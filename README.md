# 📊 Sales Performance Analysis | Excel Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

Sales Performance Analysis is a data analytics project developed using Microsoft Excel to evaluate business sales performance and compare actual revenue against monthly sales targets.

The project analyzes sales transactions, customer purchasing activity, product performance, and regional revenue to identify sales trends and highlight opportunities for business growth.

## 📊 Dataset Overview

The dataset contains information about:

- Sales transaction dates
- Customer names and purchasing activity
- Product names and unit prices
- Quantity of products sold
- Total sales revenue
- Monthly sales targets
- Weekly sales performance
- Customer locations and countries
- Regional sales distribution
- Product and customer revenue rankings

## 🔍 Key Analysis Areas

- Actual sales versus monthly targets
- Monthly revenue performance
- Weekly sales trends
- Top 3 customers by total sales
- Top 10 products by revenue
- Sales performance by region
- Sales distribution by country
- Total revenue and sales quantities
- Customer and product performance analysis
- Business target achievement
